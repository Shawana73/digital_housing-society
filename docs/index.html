Digital Housing Society

A Flutter-based mobile application designed to digitize housing society management, applicant verification, plot management and the balloting process.

Flutter · Dart · Firebase · Provider · MVVM

Introduction

About the Project

Digital Housing Society is a Final Year Project developed to make housing society operations more organized and reduce dependence on manual processes.

The application brings important administrative workflows into a digital system, including applicant records, document verification, plot management and digital balloting.

What It Offers
